# Brandscast Website Voice

## Lead With Value

Brandscast helps teams turn existing documents and updates into private audio.
The website should make that useful, tangible and easy to try. Write for someone
deciding whether the product belongs in their working day, not for an auditor.

- Lead with a benefit, then explain the feature that delivers it.
- Use everyday English, short sentences and concrete examples.
- Keep the founder-approved homepage headline: "Keep everyone informed without
  asking for their full attention."
- Audio complements email, chat, meetings and written information. Do not make
  other channels the villain or claim that internal email goes unread.
- Trial CTAs should lead to self-service signup, not prescribe a consulting
  engagement, coverage audit or sales call first.

## Put Detail Where It Helps

The September 2026 research is supporting evidence, not the voice of every page.
Do not turn heroes, benefit cards, metadata or CTAs into methodological caveats.

| Surface                      | What belongs here                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home, features, use cases    | Benefits, examples, product capabilities, next action   |
| Product FAQs and help        | Setup requirements and meaningful product limitations   |
| Analytics guide              | Metric definitions, feed attribution and interpretation |
| Research highlights          | Correct figures, named source, date and surveyed group  |
| Research sources and PDF     | Full methodology, limitations and bibliography          |
| Privacy, cookies and consent | Precise disclosures; never remove these for tone        |

For example, replace "recorded feed activity is not proof of listener identity,
comprehension or workforce reach" in a homepage benefit with "Follow listening
activity across teams and episodes to plan your next update." Explain attribution
limits in the analytics FAQ. Moving the explanation does not change the product.

## Keep The Facts

- No invented results, benchmarks, customer stories or guarantees of engagement.
- One private RSS feed per Member, containing that Member's assigned Tracks.
- Members listen in compatible podcast apps, not a Brandscast web player.
  Spotify does not accept these private RSS feeds.
- Feed links are credentials. Revocation blocks future requests; it cannot
  erase prior downloads. Explain this plainly on the access-control page.
- Listening activity is not proof of comprehension or confirmed human identity.
  Describe trends and recorded activity without treating them as business impact.
- The 30-day trial includes 5 shared AI minutes for the whole trial, not a
  recurring allowance. Paid-plan prices and allowances come from pricing.ts.
- Retain the final report PDF, direct download, sources and explicit optional
  email consent. Never add an email gate to the download.

## Review And Release

Read the whole page, including metadata, FAQs and CTA, for a consistent voice.
Keep layout and functionality unchanged in a copy-only revision. Validate with
the remote production build and desktop/mobile browser checks before closing.

Automated checks protect facts and known regressions. They must not require
verbatim disclaimers in sales copy or label every ordinary benefit a claim that
requires a research pilot. Update such checks with a documented reason rather
than distorting the copy to appease them.
